      Limitations

      This builds a reflowable EPUB, not a photocopy of the page. Text-based PDFs convert best.

      Works well

      • Text-based PDFs from Word, InDesign, LaTeX, or most ebooks
      • Documents with a clear heading size for chapter splits
      • One PDF, or a short queue; files stay on this computer

      Will not

      • Scanned PDFs — OCR runs if Tesseract is installed; otherwise pages may be kept as images
      • Password-protected files (they are rejected)
      • Uploads larger than 80 MB
      • Exact page design: columns, tables, footnotes, magazines

      Also know

      • Chapters are guessed from font size and can split in the wrong place
      • You can edit the title, author, and chapter names before converting
      • Repeated headers, footers, and page numbers are stripped heuristically
      • Tiny or decorative images are skipped; you can turn images off
      • Preview is cached for this session so convert can reuse it — it is not a quality guarantee
